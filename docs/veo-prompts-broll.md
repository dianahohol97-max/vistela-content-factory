# Veo / Gemini — b-roll пак для навчальних та POV відео

6 самодостатніх промптів: перебивки для tutorial/etiquette/POV рілсів.
Правило: екран телефона ЗАВЖДИ вимкнений/порожній — продукт вставляється
потім у композитингу (ідеальна якість скріна замість AI-каші).

Генерувати по 2–3 дублі; найкращі → Dropbox `/весільні відео`.

## 1 · Рука з телефоном на церемонії (golden hour) — головна перебивка

> Real wedding stock footage, vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. Close-up
> over-the-shoulder shot of a woman's hand with a neutral manicure holding a
> smartphone at an outdoor seaside wedding ceremony; the phone screen is
> switched off, plain dark glass. Blurred in the background: a round floral
> arch, white chairs and seated guests, golden hour light. The hand holds the
> phone steady, tilts it slightly once. Handheld camera, shallow depth of
> field, natural colors, visible film grain. Style of a Pexels wedding clip.
> No text on screen, no logos, no faces in focus. Avoid: readable phone screen
> content, glowing screen, face close-ups, oversaturated colors, CGI look.

## 2 · Рука з телефоном за святковим столом (вечір, гірлянди)

> Real wedding stock footage, vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. Close-up of a guest's
> hand holding a smartphone above an elegant wedding reception table at dusk —
> the phone screen is off, plain dark glass. On the table, softly blurred:
> white flowers, lit candles in glass holders, wine glasses catching warm
> light; string light bokeh behind. The hand slowly raises the phone as if
> about to show something to a friend. Handheld camera, shallow depth of
> field, warm tungsten light, visible film grain, muted colors. Style of a
> Pexels wedding clip. No text, no logos. Avoid: readable screen content,
> glowing screen, faces, oversaturation, CGI-looking flowers.

## 3 · Порожня церемонія біля моря — прохід (без людей, найбезпечніший)

> Real wedding stock footage, vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. Slow steady
> walk-through shot along an empty wedding ceremony aisle on a wooden seaside
> deck: white chairs, lush flower arrangements lining the aisle, scattered
> white petals, a large round floral arch at the end, calm sea and green hills
> behind. Golden daylight, no people at all. Gimbal-smooth slow forward
> movement, natural colors, visible film grain. Style of a Pexels wedding
> venue clip. No text, no logos. Avoid: people, oversaturated colors,
> CGI-looking flowers, warped geometry.

## 4 · Стіл і декор без людей — панорама

> Real wedding stock footage, vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. Slow pan across an
> elegant outdoor wedding reception table at dusk: white flowers and greenery
> down the middle, lit candles in glass holders, folded napkins, wine glasses
> catching warm light, string lights bokeh behind. No people. Gimbal-smooth
> slow lateral movement, shallow depth of field, natural colors, visible film
> grain. Style of a Pexels wedding decor clip. No text, no logos. Avoid:
> people, CGI-looking flowers, oversaturated colors, warped reflections.

## 5 · Гості дістають телефони (зі спини, без облич)

> Real wedding stock footage, vertical 9:16, 8 seconds. Filmed from behind the
> last row of seated guests at an outdoor garden wedding ceremony: several
> guests raise their phones to take photos of the floral arch ahead, phone
> screens showing only dark glass reflections. Soft daylight, backs of heads
> and shoulders only, no faces visible. Static handheld camera, natural
> colors, visible film grain. Style of a Pexels wedding ceremony clip. No
> text, no logos. Avoid: visible faces, readable phone screens, glowing
> screens, dramatic acting, oversaturation.

## 6 · Макро: конверт і печатка — НЕ ГЕНЕРУВАТИ (папір, суперечить digital-only)

> Vertical 9:16 video, 8 seconds, macro photography style. Close-up of a dusty
> blue handmade paper envelope lying on a linen tablecloth beside a sprig of
> white flowers, sealed with a matching dusty blue wax seal embossed with a
> rose. Soft diffused window light slowly shifts across the paper texture,
> gentle shadow play, shallow depth of field. Luxury wedding stationery
> aesthetic, muted editorial color grading, no text, no logos, no people, no
> hands. Avoid: oversaturated colors, CGI look, warped seal geometry, harsh
> light.

## Пам'ятка

- №1–2 найцінніші: у вимкнений екран вставляється будь-який продукт.
- Артефакти дивитись: пальці (№1–2), відображення екранів (№5).
- AI-лейбл на TikTok/IG вмикати для кліпів з людьми (№1, 2, 5).
